# Innovation Guide

## Core Mindsets
- **Bold**: Push boundaries, challenge the status quo. Kennedy's moonshot approach—choosing hard problems specifically because they demand our best.
- **Prototype**: See ideas as drafts to overcome perfectionism. Make abstract ideas concrete; accept imperfection as part of the process.
- **Curious**: Maintain strong desire to learn and explore. Drives innovation through questioning and openness to discovery.
- **Beginner**: See with fresh eyes, embrace "vuja de"—seeing the familiar as unfamiliar to find new insights.

## Main Barriers
- **Psychological**: Perfectionism, fear, self-doubt create environments where innovation can't thrive.
- **Expert Brain**: Fixed thinking patterns and myelin pathways that lock you into established solutions.
- **Limiting Beliefs**: Negative self-narratives ("I never come up with good ideas") that block creative potential.

## 5-Step Process
1. **Decide**: Choose focus area and goals. Set clear timeframes (1 month, 5 years) to direct innovation.
2. **Incubate**: Let your brain process ideas. Allow thoughts to form and connect below conscious awareness.
3. **Explore**: Gather new information and experiences. Build knowledge breadth as raw material for innovation.
4. **Associate**: Connect different ideas together. The core of creativity is combining existing elements in new ways.
5. **Serendipity**: Allow for unexpected discoveries. Create space for happy accidents and unexpected connections.

## Practical Tools
- **WMBATW**: "What might be all the ways?" A possibility expansion framework that opens thinking beyond first solutions.
- **Momentum Steps**: Make small progress consistently. Break large projects into achievable parts with accountability.
- **"I Wonder"**: Start curious conversations that open exploration without judgment or limits.
- **AEIOU**: Structured observation framework examining Activities, Environment, Interaction, Objects, Users to identify hidden needs.
- **Perspective Shifts**: Techniques like different photo angles that train your mind to see problems from multiple viewpoints.
- **Needs Analysis**: Three-layer approach identifying functional (practical), emotional (feeling), and social (perception) requirements.

## Support System
- **Environment**: Design spaces with music, color, plants, and protected time to enhance creative thinking.
- **Collaboration**: Work with others and AI to achieve what you couldn't alone. Teams outperform individuals in innovation.
- **Worksheet**: Simple two-column method to track limiting beliefs against reality, transforming mental barriers.

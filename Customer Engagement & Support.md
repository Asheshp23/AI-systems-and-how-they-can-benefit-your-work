# Customer Service Career Notes

## Finding the Right Company

### Research Before Applying
- Check if the company values service or just operations
- Read job descriptions for words like "empathetic" or "customer-oriented"
- Talk to current employees about what they like most
- Ask about company structure and key metrics
- Find out if customer satisfaction matters more than short-term profits

### Red Flags to Avoid
- Employees who complain about customers
- Job descriptions that ignore service language
- Companies focused only on speed and efficiency
- Leadership that prioritizes internal metrics over customer outcomes

## Interview Preparation

### Hard Skills First
- Prove you can do the actual job requirements
- Don't focus only on culture fit
- Show technical competency

### Soft Skills to Highlight
- Communication style with examples
- Problem-solving creativity
- Team collaboration stories
- Difficult situation management
- Empathy in action

### Interview Strategy
- Practice stories that show service mindset
- Demonstrate the skills during the interview
- Follow up with thank you and additional thoughts
- Make it easy for interviewer to recommend you

## Service Mindset Examples

### Four Companies That Get It Right
- **Amazon**: Customer-centric mission, empty chair for customer in meetings
- **Southwest Airlines**: Happy employees who go extra mile for passengers  
- **LinkedIn**: Long-term member success over product features
- **Lexus**: Dealers selected for service commitment, not just sales

### Key Takeaway
Service culture can work in any industry as a competitive advantage. The skills transfer everywhere you go.

# Business Analysis 101: Driving Change

## Why Change Matters

As a business analyst, you must explain why changes are needed. Know your project and business environment completely. Even with small tasks, understand the bigger picture.

Business analysts work as both strategists and tacticians. You drive discussions about why changes matter while also defining requirements and solutions.

## Getting Specific

Don't accept vague reasons for change. Skip past mission statements and find specific explanations like:
- "We're changing our system to attract new customers"
- "We're ending manual processes due to industry changes"

## Your Value as a BA

You represent change to stakeholders. Your discussions with customers help create solid requirements.

## Working with Stakeholders

1. Consider their viewpoints
2. Keep a question list
3. Involve them early
4. Create feedback loops
5. Hold open sessions
6. Communicate options and challenges consistently

## Process Analysis Steps

1. Find existing processes
2. Agree on naming
3. Identify outputs
4. Map connections between processes
5. Create visual process maps

## Evaluating Current Processes

Ask:
- Who does what tasks?
- How long do tasks take?
- Where are the delays?
- Are processes efficient?
- Do they give competitive advantage?

## Value Categories

- Client value
- Business value
- Non-value add

## Success Measurements

- Critical Success Factors (CSFs): Key concepts needed for success
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Metrics that track progress
- Balanced Scorecard: Views from innovation, operations, customer, and financial perspectives

## Personal Growth

Connect your career goals to transformation by:
- Seeing yourself as a change agent
- Staying adaptable
- Remaining flexible
- Building tech skills
- Collaborating effectively

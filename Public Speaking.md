# Public Speaking Guide

## Overcome Fear
- Look at audience needs first
- Reflect on your fears
- Engage with audience and adapt

## Speak Clearly
- Use simple words
- Keep sentences short
- Give clear examples
- Present ideas in logical order
- Don't assume what audience knows

## Build Your Style
- Use your natural strengths
- Add professional qualities
- Find balanced energy level
- Be yourself, but confident

## Start Strong
- Use light humor
- Make relatable statements
- Take a breath before beginning

## Virtual Tips
- Create real background (no fake/blurred)
- Look at camera
- Keep camera at eye level
- Show your hands

## Interview Success
- Show real interest
- Tell brief stories
- Watch interviewer reactions
- Change your voice tone
- Connect emotionally

## Avoid Mistakes
- Don't end statements like questions
- Remember to smile
- Speak at good pace
- Don't just read slides
- Check in with audience
- Stay calm under pressure
- Think before responding
- Let others speak
- Own your mistakes
- Find common ground in conflicts

## Myths Busted
- Drink water instead of clearing throat
- Don't imagine audience naked
- Record practice instead of using mirror
- Speak naturally, not artificially loud
- Be yourself, not an imitation
